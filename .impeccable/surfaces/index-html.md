# Surface: Yashil Vodiy launch site (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: developers' owners and marketing heads first, Tashkent buyers second. Action: choose a floor, then book a visit. Proof: the 3D story (floor, sunlight, view, schedule). Constraints: UZ/RU/EN, static site, the scene's buildings and lighting stay; UI and scene accents change. Wrong if it looks like a cheap local real-estate site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site stands under a Tashkent chinar avenue in November. It refuses the category's dark glass card, gold serif and luxury-lounge voice; the interface is bark and shade, lit by the same sun as the flat.

OWN-WORLD: Plane-tree bark camouflage (bone #dcdcc6, sage #c7c9ad, olive #a3a689) as the light ground; shade ink #10221f for text and solid panels; chinar gold #e39a2d as the only warm colour (chosen floor, active chapter, primary action); aryk teal #2c8a86 only for water, routes and "available". Geologica with sharp leaf-tip terminals for display and numbers, Golos Text for reading. Hard edges, one 1.5 px line weight, no glass, blur or gradients, 24 px module. Raises: one-colour signal rule (VU meter), one line weight (cassette j-card), hard edges with no blending (toyism), one module grid (ANSI board), deep shade bands between sections (cloud quarry).

STORY: Visitors learn they can choose the exact light they will live in, believe it because the 3D shows their floor, sun and schedule honestly, then pick a floor and book a visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The dusk scene full-bleed. A 56 px shade header with the chinar-leaf mark. On the left a solid shade panel about 440 px wide: headline "Choose the light you'll live in." in sharp Geologica about 56 px, a two-line lede, a tabular facts row, a gold "Choose your floor" button that jumps to the picker, and a quiet "Book a visit" link. Hard-edged leaf sunflecks cross the panel. Signature interaction: the sunflecks move and fade with the 3D sun's azimuth and height in every chapter, and are strongest in the sunlight study.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 (chinar avenue, the literal reading of the name) from my ordered list; seed:9be5d1bdf72cd41b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
